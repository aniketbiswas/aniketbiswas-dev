Assumptions, fee rules & what is not modelled

A model, not a bank quote

This India-focused home-loan calculator uses illustrative defaults, not current lender offers or a personal recommendation. It assumes a fully disbursed loan, monthly interest at annual rate ÷ 1,200, payments at month end and extras after the EMI. The original EMI is retained when rates change or principal is prepaid.

It does not model daily accrual, construction-stage disbursements, missed payments, lender rounding, tax benefits, investment returns or property appreciation. Check all actual terms with your lender.

Prepayment charges: check the loan type

The RBI’s 2025 Pre-payment Charges Directions apply to loans sanctioned or renewed on or after 1 January 2026. Floating-rate loans to individuals for non-business purposes generally have no part/full prepayment charge, regardless of the source of funds and without a lock-in period.

For hybrid loans, floating-rate status at prepayment matters. For fixed-rate loans, check the terms. Enter charges only when applicable to your loan; zero defaults do not mean every charge is waived.

Fees and your data

Processing percentage and fixed fees are additive. The entered fee-tax percentage applies to processing, annual and prepayment charges, not loan principal or interest. Other upfront charges are already all-in. The 18% default is an editable example only.
